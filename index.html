Privacy Policy

Cyber Punk Bubble Shooter
Commokazi Studios
Last updated: 23 November 2026

The short version

The game collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, no network requests after install.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your high score and your music on/off preference locally using the browser storage of the app. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What is not collected

  • No name, email or account
  • No location
  • No device identifiers
  • No usage analytics
  • No crash reporting
  • No advertising identifiers

Permissions

The app requests vibration only, used for haptic feedback while playing. It requests no camera, microphone, storage, contacts or location access.

Third parties

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect install and crash data under its own privacy policy, which is outside our control. See policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change and the new version will be published at this URL.
